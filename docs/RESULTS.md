# Results and uses of the pilot

The resource connects 162,914 public chemical recipes with 190,699 compound-context profiles across eight contexts. RNA responses are represented on 6,000 genes and 32 shared programs. Checkpoints, explicit matrix axes, fixed folds and four worked notebooks support direct model development. [Start Here](../START_HERE.md).

| Result | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---|
| Recipe-to-program prediction | Mean per-program Pearson 0.105–0.530 across eight contexts; exact model/split selectors in the [quantitative reference](PILOT_RESULTS_REFERENCE.md) |
| Response-family atlas | 1,007 keyed clusters and 5,346 memberships; 855 clusters with significant pathway annotations; [members and centroids](../atlas/original_clusters/README.md) |
| HSPA5-associated family | 1,512 filtered measured compounds, 5,373 cells, member-half aggregate-profile r = 0.981; response correspondence provides a mechanism hypothesis; [family example](../annex_case_studies/01_hspa5_building_block/README.md) |
| Partner comparison | Aggregate groups differ in reference preference; matched support sensitivity is inconclusive; [comparison and limitations](../annex_case_studies/02_partner_responses/README.md) |
| Retrospective selection | At 1,400 selections, HSPA5 high-score-set recovery averages 44.54% for Updating model, 34.41% for Frozen model and 15.73% for Random selection over three starts; [endpoint and curves](../annex_case_studies/03_adaptive_replay/README.md) |
| Exact image/RNA pairing | At 8,000 training wells, residual RNA-coordinate correlation 0.1248 for exact pairs versus 0.0007 for shuffled pairs; mean R² 0.0029 versus −0.0309; [measurement design](../annex_measurement_design/README.md) |
| Control batch accumulation | Explicit acquisition/reference partitions show how agreement changes with additional batches; [tables and partition sensitivity](../annex_measurement_design/README.md) |
| Microscopy | Two five-channel observations with public compound IDs and compound-linked RNA profiles; [gallery and reproducible selection](../gallery/README.md) |

Use the [data dictionary](DATA_DICTIONARY.md) for keys and axes, the [annex index](ANNEX_INDEX.md) for measurement units, and [methods](METHODS.md) for normalization and evaluation. Genetic resemblance and family coherence do not establish direct molecular target engagement.
